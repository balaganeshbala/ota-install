📱 Installation & Trust Steps

  1. Install the app: Tap the install button above
  2. Wait for download: The app will download and appear on your home screen
  3. Trust the developer:
    • Go to Settings → General → VPN & Device Management
    • Under "Enterprise App", find and tap on the developer profile
    • Tap "Trust [Developer Name]"
    • Confirm by tapping "Trust" again
  4. Launch the app: Return to home screen and open the app
